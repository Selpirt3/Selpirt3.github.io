Research

Connecting solar eruptions to exoplanetary space weather.

01

Mechanism of Solar Eruptions

Solar eruptions, including flares and coronal mass ejections (CMEs), are the primary drivers of space weather near Earth. We investigate their properties and underlying mechanisms.

02

Solar-Stellar Connection

Using the Sun as a benchmark, we explore how CMEs occur on other stars, how their properties vary across different stellar types, and how they can be identified through observations.

03

Exoplanetary Space Weather

Through numerical simulions, we investigate how stellar CMEs on different stars shape exoplanetary space-weather environments and assess their impact on the habitability.
